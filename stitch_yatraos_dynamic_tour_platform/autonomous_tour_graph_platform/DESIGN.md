## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, editorial luxury aesthetic tailored for high-end autonomous itinerary intelligence and geospatial graph operations. Moving away from sterile, cold-cyan enterprise dashboards, it embraces the tactile warmth of artisanal paper, rich roasted biscoff caramel, and espresso depth.

The interface evokes confidence, quiet precision, and worldly craftsmanship. The design blends **Editorial Warm Luxury** with **Subtle Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Soft-lit layered biscuit surfaces simulate heavyweight uncoated parchment.
- Glass panels carry warm amber-caramel refractions rather than cold synthetic blurs.
- Micro-tactile borders and quiet inner specular highlights produce depth that feels physical yet calibrated for complex graph visualizations, timeline orchestrations, and travel intelligence feeds.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in culinary warmth, artisanal papermaking, and grounding earth tones.

### Core Tones
- **Primary (Biscoff Caramel):** `#8C532B` (deep caramelized speculoos) anchors primary actions, key nodes, active tabs, and emphasized links. Complementary values `#A56336` and `#C88A58` serve as interactive hover states, active paths, and gradient accents.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Bronze):** `#B85C38` offers refined contrast for telemetry tags, route switches, and focal journey landmarks.
- **Tertiary (Warm Golden Biscuit):** `#C88A58` functions as secondary graph edges, hover accents, and informational rings.

### Neutral Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` (clean warm porcelain cream).
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Panel/Card):** `#FAF6EE` (delicate folded biscuit).
- **Surface Level 2 (Inset/Muted Container):** `#F6EFE6` (warm oat grain).
- **Surface Level 3 (Borders/Dividers):** `#ECE1D0` (pressed artisan fiber).
- **High-Contrast Text Primary:** `#1B1510` (deepest espresso charcoal, 13.5:1 contrast against `#FDFBF7`).
- **High-Contrast Text Secondary:** `#4A3E36` (roasted chicory, 7.2:1 contrast against `#FAF6EE`).
- **Muted Text / Metadata:** `#7A6C62` (warm stone brown).

### Semantic & Operational Signals
- **Operational Positive (Soft Sage Emerald):** `#2D6A4F` for successful autonomous recalculations, on-time arrivals, and validated nodes.
- **Operational Precaution / Risk (Warm Ochre Amber):** `#D97706` for weather deviations, schedule tight windows, and pending authorizations.
- **Operational Critical Disruption (Vermilion):** `#C2410C` for road closures, node disconnects, and manual intervention alerts.

## Typography

Typographic scale is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, utilizing its geometric clarity, friendly open counters, and sculpted humanist curves to provide high editorial polish.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set tight letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with semi-bold to bold weights to evoke modern luxury publishing headlines and bespoke travel journals.
- **Data & Telemetry:** Numbers within node telemetry, graph metrics, and duration indicators rely on the tabular figure features (`tnum`) of Plus Jakarta Sans to prevent jitter in animated autonomous updates.
- **Labels & Overlays:** The `label-caps` variant is applied to status indicators, coordinates, map pin metadata, and system alerts to maintain legibility at glanceable micro-scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an adaptive 12-column grid system paired with split viewport orchestration (graph visualizer on canvas, contextual panels docked or floating).

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 40px (`2.5rem`) outer margins. Floating overlay panels use a fixed footprint of 420px to preserve maximum map/graph real estate.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 24px margins. Context panels dock to an off-canvas drawer or a 380px collapsible rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) margins. Journey segments, waypoints, and telemetry transform into vertical bottom sheets with snap points at 25%, 50%, and 90% viewport height.
- **Rhythm Principle:** Component internals follow a 4px baseline (`0.25rem`), while card gutters and inter-section modules space evenly at multiples of 12px and 24px (`space-sm`, `space-lg`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2** (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for medium surfaces, `1.5rem` for modals and graph cards).

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `1rem` (`16px`) radius creates an organic, smooth stone aesthetic without appearing overly bubbly or casual.
- **Pills & Tags:** Telemetry pills, graph node chips, and category tags utilize full capsule radius (`9999px`) to distinguish actionable node chips from structural layout cards.
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** Consistent `0.5rem` to `0.75rem` rounding ensures crisp touch boundaries.
- **Graph Nodes:** Concentric circular geometries with dual rings (outer pulse ring in `tertiary` tone, inner anchor in `#8C532B`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Autonomous Deploy / Optimize):** Rich caramel gradient from `#8C532B` to `#A56336`, text `#FDFBF7`, subtle top inner highlight `inset 0px 1px 0px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`. Hover elevates shadow to `0px 6px 20px rgba(140, 83, 43, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Action:** `#F6EFE6` surface, border `1px solid #ECE1D0`, text `#1B1510`. Hover transitions background to `#FAF6EE` with border `#C88A58`.
- **Ghost / Map Control:** Translucent biscuit (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)`), text `#241C15`, icon-driven with micro-shadow.

### Chips & Node Badges
- **Waypoint / Node Chip:** Capsule-shaped, padding `4px 12px`.
  - Base: background `#F6EFE6`, border `1px solid #ECE1D0`, text `#241C15`.
  - Active: background `#8C532B`, text `#FDFBF7`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Status Indicator Badges:**
  - *Optimized / Live:* Background `rgba(45, 106, 79, 0.12)`, text `#2D6A4F`, dot indicator `#2D6A4F`.
  - *Reroute Calculated / Warning:* Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.14)`, text `#D97706`, dot indicator `#D97706`.
  - *Node Disruption:* Background `rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.12)`, text `#C2410C`, dot indicator `#C2410C`.

### Form Fields & Search Inputs
- **Graph Search & Route Builder Inputs:** Height 44px, background `#FDFBF7`, border `1px solid #ECE1D0`, text `#1B1510`, placeholder `#7A6C62`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#8C532B` with a soft ambient outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(140, 83, 43, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radios
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Unchecked border `1.5px solid #C88A58`, background `#FAF6EE`. Checked state `#8C532B` with crisp `#FDFBF7` checkmark/pip.
- **Autonomous Mode Switch (Toggle):** Track in `#ECE1D0` (off) to `#8C532B` (on). Thumb in `#FDFBF7` with tactile micro-drop shadow (`0 2px 4px rgba(27, 21, 16, 0.15)`).

### Cards & Graph Inspectors
- **Itinerary Node Card:** Resting background `#FAF6EE`, `16px` padding, border `1px solid #ECE1D0`. Displays sequential timestamp, waypoint title in bold espresso `#1B1510`, and terracotta route tags.
- **Selected Card:** Outer border shifts to `1.5px solid #8C532B` with a warm specular tint (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(200, 138, 88, 0.2)`).

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Autonomous Graph Flow Line:** SVG Bézier connections rendered in `#C88A58` with dynamic particle pulses in `#8C532B` denoting active autonomous vehicle or itinerary transit.
- **Timeline Breadcrumb Rail:** Left-docked vertical track in `#ECE1D0` with biscoff caramel circular nodes indicating departure, transit, check-in, and curated experience waypoints.
