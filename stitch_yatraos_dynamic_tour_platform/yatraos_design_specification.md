# YatraOS — Design System & Product Design Architecture
**Personalized Dynamic Tour Planning & Tour Operations Platform (HackCelestial 3.0 · PS ID 7)**

## Core Aesthetic & Creative Direction: "Cosmic Cartography & Mission Control"
- **Tone:** Ultra-premium, production-grade, dark-mode-first aesthetic with luminescent telemetry accents (Emerald #10B981 for healthy nodes, Vivid Amber #F59E0B for risk/delayed nodes, Electric Crimson #EF4444 for disruptions, and Radiant Indigo/Cyan #6366F1 / #06B6D4 for AI graph links).
- **Typography:** Display typography with geometric precision (Outfit / Plus Jakarta Sans) paired with mono-spaced numeric telemetry for coordinates, timestamps, and cost deltas (JetBrains Mono).
- **Surface Elevation:** Deep obsidian canvas (`#090D16`), glassmorphic panels with subtle glowing borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), multi-layered backdrop blurs, and interactive SVG node graphs.

## Multi-Door Design Architecture
1. **Flagship Showcase & Marketing Portal:** Interactive product experience highlighting the "Dependency Graph vs. Flat Itinerary" paradigm, live interactive Ripple Simulator, live architecture telemetry, and instant portal switchers.
2. **Traveller Live Graph & Autonomous Recovery Cockpit:** Dynamic interactive day-by-day canvas where bookings are connected dependency nodes (train -> hotel -> sunset boat), featuring live simulated disruption with the 3 ranked recovery options (Cheapest, Fastest, Least-Disruptive) and live group ledger split.
3. **Operator Control Room & Risk Radar:** High-density command center with real-time Ripple Impact Score (0-100) sorting, fleet-wide disruption radar, vendor slot reassignment matrix, and automated emergency action logs.
